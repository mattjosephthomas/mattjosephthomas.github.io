Parkbus

I run a tourism business.

I understand this industry because I'm the CEO of Parkbus. My team and I do this work every day.

At Parkbus, we connect people to outdoor experiences across Canada. Over the years, we've served more than 300,000 passengers, built partnerships with Parks Canada and VIA Rail, and grown revenue by roughly 30% two years running.

We operate across six Canadian cities with a lean full-time team. It's a real business with real customers, real operating challenges and real economics to think about.

Our work with Parks Canada and VIA Rail reflects something we believe: tourism works better when the right organizations work together to make experiences easier to discover and access.

Everything else I build comes from that experience. I see the problems firsthand, develop practical solutions, and put them to work.

Why it matters

Small businesses can make big profits.

I think tourism is a great industry to be in. You can build a business around a place, an experience or something people genuinely love doing.

You shouldn't need a huge team or a complicated operation to do it.

Technology can help with all of this, but only when it's solving a real problem.

The best small businesses have a few things in common.
  • They sell something people want.
  • They understand their numbers.
  • They price their products properly.
  • They know how to reach customers.
  • They have systems that let the business run without every decision landing on the owner's desk.

More software isn't the goal.

More automation isn't the goal.

Even growth isn't always the goal.

The goal is a better business.

One that's more profitable, easier to operate and worth owning.

That's the thinking behind everything I build.
