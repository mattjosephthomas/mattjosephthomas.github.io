About me.

I'm the CEO of Parkbus, Canada's national outdoor travel network. For more than 15 years, Parkbus has connected people to national and provincial parks, with routes from Halifax to Vancouver, helping destinations turn access into visitation. Most recently, we launched overnight trips through Visit Canada Parks, developed in partnership with VIA Rail and Parks Canada.

I'm also the co-founder of Whereabouts, a CRM built for destination marketing organizations. After years of watching DMOs struggle with tools that were too complex, too expensive or not designed for how tourism works, we built something simpler. Today it supports DMOs across North America.

Before building companies, I spent a decade inside the tourism system, supporting marketing and development with organizations like Destination Ontario, Indigenous Tourism Ontario, Kawartha Lakes, Prince Edward County and Visit Nacogdoches, Texas. That taught me why so many good strategies stall. They aren't wrong. No one builds what's needed to carry them out.

There are many brilliant people focused on destination marketing and strategy. I'm not trying to compete with them. I focus on what happens after the strategy: operations, partnerships, technology, financing, and the unglamorous backbone that decides whether places thrive or just keep planning to.

I hold a Master's in Strategic Foresight & Innovation from OCAD University and a certificate in Cultural Planning from UBC. Earlier, I barely graduated from the University of Ottawa, which taught me that credentials matter less than doing the work. Along the way, I was part of a team named Innovator of the Year at the Ontario Tourism Summit.
