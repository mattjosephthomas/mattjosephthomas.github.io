Good businesses deserve better systems.

I think small tourism businesses can be some of the best businesses to own. They can have loyal customers, distinctive products and real value in the places where they operate.

But too many owners spend their days buried in administration, working around outdated systems or trying to do the job of five people.

There's often more profit hiding in the business they already have. Sometimes that means improving pricing. Sometimes it's packaging a better product, fixing the website, improving conversion or automating work that shouldn't need to be done manually.

The opportunity isn't always to grow bigger. It's to make the business work better.

How I help

  1. Find the opportunity

    Identify where time and money are being wasted, and where the business could be making more.

  2. Fix the system

    Improve the offer, pricing, marketing, processes and technology.

  3. Make it work in practice

    Put the changes in place with the people who will use them, measure the results and refine what doesn't work.

I take on a limited number of consulting and implementation projects with tourism businesses where there's a real opportunity to improve profitability.

I'm particularly interested in businesses with good products, ambitious owners and the potential to become much better businesses without becoming much bigger ones.
